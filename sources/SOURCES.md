shows the attribution under "Drawing sources" beneath the spec table.

Notes:
- b777family.png holds both 777-200ER and 777-300ER views; the 777-300ER uses its own side and
  top views and the 777-200ER front view (same wing apart from the raked tips).
- g500.svg is the GV-SP-based Gulfstream G500 of 2003, externally identical to the G550.
- The 574x385 PNGs (F/A-18, B-1B, AH-64) and the B-2 drawing originate from U.S. Army field
  manual FM 44-80 (public domain); the CH-47 SVG is Jetijones' redraw of the same series.
- typhoon.svg shows the aircraft with external stores, which remain in the silhouette.
- Drawings with the undercarriage drawn detached (Kaboldy style) are traced gear-up and sit on a
  ground offset derived from the official height.
- Family sheets (757, 767, 727, ATR, A330/A340 are single-type) hold several variants; the config
  crops the variant named in the aircraft JSON. The 767-300ER uses the 767-200 front view.
- b747family.png holds one top and front view (747-100) and several side views; the 747-8I entry
  uses its own side view with the 747-100 top/front scaled to 747-8 dimensions (approximate).
- mustang3.svg shows the razorback P-51B/C (Mustang III) and stands in for the P-51 entry.
- Derived variants (no 3-view on Commons): the 787-9 and 787-10 are the 787-8 drawing with
  Boeing's fuselage plugs inserted (`stretch` in tools/sources.json); wing, tail and front view
  are the 787-8's, as on the real aircraft. The A350-900 and A350-1000 use the public-domain A350
  side profiles (a350-sides.svg) for the side view, and the A330-300 drawing stretched to A350
  length and scaled to A350 span for the top and front views; these two are approximations and
  are flagged as such in their JSON notes.
